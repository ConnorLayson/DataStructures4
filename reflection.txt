The stack is the perfect choice for an undo and redo system because you want to undo the most recent 
actions first, as opposed to removing the first thing you wrote on your document. It would be counter-
productive to remove the very first sentance of your book report, when the only part you messed up
is the last sentance, and if we used a queue for our undos, we would be doing exactly that, but 
with a stack system, we are doing exactly what we want by removing the last thing added. 

The queue is best for a help desk because these are supposed to represent people waiting in a line,
and if these are real people (like they would be in a help ticket system), then it would be unfair to 
the person who submitted their ticket first, as they would be the last person to be helpped if we used a 
stack. Not to mention that if anyone submitted more help tickets, that first person would only get pushed
farther and farther back.

These differ from python's built-in lists by not storing every value seperatly, but only storing the
most important values' neighbors, and just re-assigning who's at the front of the line/top of the stack.
This gives less controll over everything, like the order they are in, but in a use case like this, you
don't really need to manipulate the order of anything.
